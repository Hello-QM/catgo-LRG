反应: Oxygen Reduction Reaction (氧还原反应)
路径: O2 → *OOH → *O + H2O → *OH + H2O → 2H2O
表面: Pt(1, 1, 1)

结构列表 (pathway.traj中的顺序):
----------------------------------------
0: slab (干净表面)
1: O2 (bridge, lying)
2: OOH (ontop, upright)
3: O (hollow, upright)
4: OH (ontop, upright)
5: H2O (ontop, upright)
----------------------------------------

读取方法:
  from ase.io import read
