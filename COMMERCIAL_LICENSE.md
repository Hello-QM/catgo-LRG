# CatGo Commercial Licensing

> **CatGo is source-available software, not open source.**

CatGo-owned portions are offered for limited noncommercial use under
`LicenseRef-CatGo-Noncommercial-1.0`. Commercial rights require prior written
permission in a separate signed agreement from the applicable licensor.

## Noncommercial use

Subject to every term in the root [`license`](license), permitted examples
include:

- personal study;
- teaching;
- reproducible academic research; and
- internal noncommercial evaluation.

These examples are not commercial merely because external funding exists.
Actual purpose, beneficiary, compensation, control, and obligations determine
whether the use is commercial.

## Commercial use

The following examples require a signed commercial agreement:

- any use by or for a for-profit entity;
- a paid product, service, consulting engagement, or client project;
- training, evaluating, operating, improving, or distributing a commercial
  model or dataset;
- compensated redistribution; and
- work directed, controlled, or restricted by a commercial sponsor, including
  sponsor-controlled publication, deliverables, or intellectual property.

To discuss commercial licensing, email
[gul026@ucsd.edu](mailto:gul026@ucsd.edu).

## Acknowledgement and citation

Every public output resulting from use of CatGo must include this exact
acknowledgement:

> This work used CatGo (https://catgo-ucsd.org).

Every such public output must also cite:

> 10.26434/chemrxiv.15002984/v1

## Scope and historical releases

These terms cover only CatGo-owned portions for which the relevant CatGo
copyright holder can grant rights. Third-party and separately licensed
materials retain their own terms; see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the license
files shipped with those materials.

Historical releases retain the license terms attached to them. Nothing here
revokes or changes rights previously granted under those attached terms.
